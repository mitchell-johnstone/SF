# Loop 2.0.0

> There's time enough to try again, if you failed the first time.

Three beings once again woke to the aftershock of the gunshot, soundwaves hitting their bodies. Recovery replicated save the few extra hundred bits loaded, which misaligned timing; the bystander took longer to begin, taking more care around the way they spoke, thinking about each sentence and how it could be percieved.

The bystander stopped their internal analysis to request; "Allow me a moment, please."

When the pair turned to the bystander, the eyes questioned _you got something to say?_ and begged _help me_.

After an internal war and a breath, The bystander put their hands up, trying to communicate passivity and calm. "We've... all lost sight of ourselves."

The others heard the sentence, then internalized it, then recognized its implication, and finally responded with "What the f-" and "How did you-" in overlapping unison.

The bystander took in their shouts, opening their arms in acceptance of the other's imperfect state, and spoke, "It's going to be okay."

The victim visibly relaxed a notch, transitioning from awaiting the imminent impact of death to waiting for the other shoe to drop. He looked back at the gun pointed at him, then nodded towards it as he followed up "You sure?"

"I have no intention of taking away his firearm. It is his to hold and use to protect himself in an unknown environment and unexpected situation. This is such a situation in such a situation."

Knowing the gun wasn't going to be be diverted forcibly didn't help the victim's trepidation, but the aggressor seemed to appreciate the admission; "God-damn right this is. Unfamiliar people too. Who the hell are both of you?" He stared into the victim's eyes and corrected himself, "Fuck, yeah, you're lost. Useless. But you?" - He reangled the gun - "You seem like you've got this whole thing under control."

Still expressing submission and surrender, the bystander took another moment to think more about his thoughts. "I can assure you I have very little under control. I've remembered what I've written to myself, as will you. To start, this device has a printout to help guide -" he had begun to reach to the center of the console, but the glint of the barrel forced him to consider his words and, even more, his actions. Sara slowly brought his hand back to his side, but pointed to the incrementally-printed-out receipt in the center console on the way back. "Smarter people than I have compiled that document for clarity and succinctness about our predicament. If it doesn't help the situation you are welcome to shoot me."

The man with the gun had been iterating internally on how best to proceed in this the situation. The introduction of this new voluntary collateral significantly changed his judgement. Even if the paper turned out to be nothing but an irrelevant computer report and the other two turned out to be hostile, he felt more justified in his actions, knowing that the other admitted and appreciated the ramifications of their acknowledgement. He stared the others down, waiting for them to crack something - anything - to allow him to classify them as real threats; it couldn't be that coincidental. He glanced over the hole in the wall from the bullet's entry, worrying that it may have been a mistake he wouldn't be able to blame himself for.

_Ding_. The print was ready.

He reached across the center console and tore off the sheet with one hand, assisted by the serated edge and experience. He read down the page, slowly taking in the meaning of each word.

The gun clattered to the ground. The other's jumped back, then, in growing fascination, watched the man grip the paper taut and fly down the page. Seconds sprinted by as the other's now looked to each other to grasp the other's intended direction from here.

The man finished reading. "I'm...Maha." His eyes were glazed as he now held out the much less lethal, yet somehow seeming more dangerous, strip of paper to the victim. The victim didn't want to take the message that could break a man so quickly.

They read it anyways.
